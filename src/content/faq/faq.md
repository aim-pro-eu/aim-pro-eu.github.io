---
questions:
  - title: What is AIM-PRO?
    answer: AIM-PRO stands for AI Literacy for Multidisciplinary Professional
      Readiness and Outreach. It is a European Erasmus+ Alliance for Innovation
      project that aims to strengthen AI literacy across higher education,
      vocational education and training (VET) and the workforce. It develops
      innovative educational frameworks, tools and resources to help learners
      and educators engage critically and responsibly with artificial
      intelligence.
  - title: What is the main goal of AIM-PRO?
    answer: |-
      The main goal of AIM-PRO is to improve AI literacy across Europe by: 

      * Developing multidisciplinary learning frameworks 
      * Creating open educational resources (OERs) 
      * Supporting educators and learners with digital tools 
      * Promoting responsible and ethical use of AI 
      * Strengthening digital, green and entrepreneurial skills.
  - title: Who are the partners involved in AIM-PRO?
    answer: AIM-PRO is implemented by a consortium of 15 partner organisations from
      10 European countries, bringing together universities, research centres,
      training providers, certification bodies and industry partners. This
      multidisciplinary collaboration ensures that the project's AI literacy
      frameworks, tools and learning resources address the needs of education,
      training and the labour market across Europe. Details about the consortium
      can be found on the [Partners page](https://aim-pro.eu/#partners).
  - title: How is AIM-PRO funded?
    answer: AIM-PRO is funded by the European Union through the Erasmus+ Alliances
      for Innovation programme (Grant Agreement No. 101245864). The project also
      receives financial support from Movetia, the Swiss funding agency for
      exchange, mobility and cooperation in education, continuing education and
      youth work in Switzerland, Europe and worldwide. Together, these funding
      bodies support AIM-PRO's mission to strengthen AI literacy and promote
      responsible AI adoption across Europe.
