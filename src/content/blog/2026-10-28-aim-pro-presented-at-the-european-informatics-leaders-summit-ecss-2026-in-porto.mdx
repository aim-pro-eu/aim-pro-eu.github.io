AIM-PRO was presented at the **[European Informatics Leaders Summit (ECSS 2026)](https://www.informatics-europe.org/ecss-2026/home.html)**, held in Porto, Portugal, from 26 to 28 October 2026. The summit brings together leaders of informatics departments, research institutions and industry from across Europe to discuss the future of the discipline, its education and its research agenda.

The project was featured in the **[Education Research Workshop](https://www.informatics-europe.org/ecss-2026/program/education-research-workshop.html)**, in the session dedicated to **AI in Informatics Higher Education**. There, Antonio Bucchiarone (University of L’Aquila), Scientific Coordinator of AIM-PRO, delivered the presentation *“AIM-PRO: Building AI Literacy for Multidisciplinary Professional Readiness and Outreach”*.

The talk introduced the vision and objectives of the project, explaining how AIM-PRO aims to strengthen AI literacy across three complementary contexts: higher education, vocational education and training (VET), and small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). A central message of the presentation was the need to develop AI as a **tool that supports thinking, learning and professional readiness**, rather than one that undermines critical thinking and human skills. This perspective is at the core of the AIM-PRO approach to competence frameworks, open educational resources and the project’s forthcoming Digital Twin for educators and learners.

The session offered a valuable opportunity to exchange views with educators and researchers from informatics faculties across Europe on how institutions are responding to the rapid adoption of AI in teaching and learning, and on which competencies learners and professionals need in order to use AI responsibly and effectively. The discussion confirmed the relevance of AIM-PRO’s goals and opened new contacts for future collaboration and dissemination.
