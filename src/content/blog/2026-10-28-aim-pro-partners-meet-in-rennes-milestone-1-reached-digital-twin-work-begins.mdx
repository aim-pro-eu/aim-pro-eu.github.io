On 20 and 21 October 2026 the AIM-PRO consortium came together in Rennes, France, for its second project meeting (PM2). Two intense days of work allowed partners to take stock of the first months of activity and to set the direction for the next phase of the project.

The meeting opened with a review of the work packages, during which the consortium confirmed that **Milestone 1 has been reached** on schedule. The first results on the AI literacy competence framework and on the Open Educational Resources (OER) approach were presented and discussed, with contributions from all partners.

The central session of the meeting was devoted to the **Digital Twin for Educators and Learners in AI Literacy**, one of the project’s most innovative outcomes. Partners worked in groups to define user stories for the three target contexts of AIM-PRO, Higher Education, Vocational Education and Training (VET) and SMEs, and to sketch the learner and educator journeys that the Digital Twin will support, including the role of AI agents in personalising learning paths. This collaborative design work will feed directly into the technical specification and the first prototype.

The meeting also addressed project management, quality assurance and dissemination, with partners agreeing on the next steps and responsibilities for the coming months.

AIM-PRO (AI Literacy for Multidisciplinary Professional Readiness and Outreach) is an Erasmus+ project that aims to equip students, professionals and educators with the AI literacy skills needed in today’s multidisciplinary workplaces. The consortium thanks the local hosts for their warm welcome and looks forward to the next meeting.
