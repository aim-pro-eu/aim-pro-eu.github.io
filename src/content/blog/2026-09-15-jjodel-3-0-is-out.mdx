Building a modeling language usually means learning a toolchain before you can draw a single box. Jjodel 3.0 removes that step.

Released on **15 September 2026**: Jjodel 3.0, open source, browser based, nothing to install.

What is new:

* Three new textual languages: JjEL for expressions, JjTL for transformations, JjScript for refactorings and in-place transformations
* Data Manager: tables and forms for blended modeling, not just diagrams
* View Designer: notation authored from panels, no code to render your own syntax
* Jjodie: an AI assistant that creates metamodels and models from any multimodal description in a mediated way (no artifact is manipulated directly)

The point is not the feature list. It is the loop: define a language, create models, see results instantly, in the same tab.

We built it at the [**Università degli Studi dell'Aquila**](https://www.linkedin.com/school/universit-degli-studi-dell'aquila/) in the [**SWEN research group**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/swen-research-group/), for research and for teaching. If you teach metamodeling and model-driven engineering, we would like to know what your students get stuck on first.
