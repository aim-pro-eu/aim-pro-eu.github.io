AIM-PRO was in Lanciano (Abruzzo, Italy) for the Abruzzo Edition of **[Fiera Didacta Italia](https://fieradidacta.indire.it/en/visit-didacta-italia-abruzzo-edition/)**, the country’s leading fair dedicated to education, training and innovation in schools. The project contributed to the programme with the teacher-training seminar **“Using AI to Learn AI”**, addressed to teachers of lower- and upper-secondary schools.

During the seminar, Antonio Bucchiarone, Scientific Coordinator of AIM-PRO, presented the project and its vision of a **Digital Twin for education and professional training**. The idea is deliberately different from the AI assistants that have become familiar in classrooms: not a system that hands out answers, but a living model of the learning path, continuously updated as teacher and student move forward, and able to give each of them targeted guidance. For teachers, the Digital Twin suggests pedagogical strategies, scenarios, resources and tailored activities; for students, it offers a personalised “training ground” in which to practise and progress.

The session combined this vision with **hands-on examples of how AI tools** can be used to understand AI itself, its possibilities, its limits and the critical-thinking skills needed to use it responsibly, in line with AIM-PRO’s goal of strengthening AI literacy across higher education, vocational education and training, and the professional world.

The exchange with participants was one of the highlights of the day. Teachers brought questions and ideas from their everyday classroom experience, confirming a principle at the heart of the project: AI literacy is built together with those who teach every day. Their feedback will inform the design of the project’s educational resources and of the Digital Twin itself.
