Last week, from October 4 to 9, the **ACM/IEEE 29th International Conference on Model-Driven Engineering Languages and Systems (MODELS)** was held in Málaga, Spain, attended by a group of researchers affiliated with the project: *Riccardo Belliato* (FBK), *Antonio Bucchiarone*, *Juri di Rocco* (Univaq), *Judith Michaels*, *Jakob Steffens*, *Michael Mittermaier* (UREG) and *Antonio Cicchetti* (MDU).

During the **Educators Symposium** (the workshop on Model-Driven Engineering in Education) and during the Poster Session, Antonio and Riccardo presented our last published paper: *A Pedagogy-Aware Model-Driven Engineering Approach for AI Literacy*. In this paper we presented our Model-driven pedagogy-first approach to AI Literacy.

The paper (readable at [https://doi.org/10.1145/3837062.3838920]()) shows how we represented the domain of AI Literacy (Learning Scenarios, Competencies, Assessments, etc.) through meta models and how link them to represent tailored Learning Paths for our stakeholders in various context, and how Jjodel (the meta modeling platform we used developed by Univaq) and Jjodie (the AI Assistant integrated in Jjodel) can assists non-MDE experts in modeling them.

In addition to the paper and the presentation, the authors also created a poster, which was presented the following day (October 7th) at the Poster Session of the Conference.

The conference also provided a valuable opportunity to exchange feedback and gather ideas from the MDE and Digital Twins communities for the project's next steps.

Thanks to our partners and the MODELS Organization Committee for for the success of this work. Stay tuned for our nexts milestones.
