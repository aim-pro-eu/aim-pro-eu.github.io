The European project AIM-PRO presented at Greece’s [Regional Growth Conference 2026](https://www.rgc.gr/en/) in Patras as part of its dissemination efforts. The conference provided an opportunity to present the project’s main objectives, research approach and current actions. Special attention was given to the use of innovative technologies and interdisciplinary methodologies to address contemporary developmental and social challenges. 

The conference also underscored the importance of collaboration among academia, research organisations, public administration, vocational education and the innovation ecosystem. Such partnerships are essential for driving sustainable and technologically advanced solutions with a regional and social impact. 

AIM-PRO's participation in the workshop **["From Artificial Intelligence to the Skills of the Future"](https://www.rgc.gr/en/edu-acts-2/)**, held under the Erasmus+ Alliances for Innovation - [Generative AI Skills Academy (GenAISA)](https://genaisa.eu/) project, further contributed to the strengthening of its networ. The event facilitated knowledge exchange and the sharing of best practice with representatives from the scientific, research, educational and business communities. 

The AIM-PRO team warmly thanks the conference and workshop organizers  for the opportunity to present the project in such a distinguished setting of scientific and developmental dialogue.
