Our Scientific Coordinator, Antonio Bucchiarone (University of L’Aquila), introduced AIM-PRO during the teacher-training seminar “**Using AI to Learn AI**” at Didacta Italia 2026 – Abruzzo Edition. In his talk he presented the Erasmus+ project and its vision for **AI-supported education and teacher training**.
